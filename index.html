Gastronomy

A Table Set for the Extraordinary

At Lumière, our kitchen follows the coast and the season. Menus are written each morning around what arrives from the market and the tide — served beneath low light, in a room of oak, brass and linen.

Breakfast is served until eleven. Dinner begins at six, and the last table is held for those who prefer the quiet end of the evening.

Wellness

Stillness, By Design

Below the courtyard, the spa keeps its own hours and its own hush. Stone, water and warm oil — treatments drawn from the Mediterranean tradition, given time to work.

The thermal suite, lap pool and relaxation terrace are open to resident guests from six in the morning until ten at night.
